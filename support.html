CUSTOMER + BUSINESS
How do I join a business loyalty program?+

Open the AP RESERVE Loyalty customer app and use the business's membership or joining QR code, following the options shown in the app. Each business has its own membership and points balance.

Can I combine points from different businesses?+

No. Each participating business runs its own loyalty program. Points, reward rates, and redemption terms are kept separate.

Who can correct my points balance?+

The participating business issues, adjusts, and redeems its own points. For a missing or incorrect balance, please contact that business first.

How do I change the rewards percentage?+

In the AP RESERVE Business app, open your business settings and find the rewards configuration. Your chosen rate controls how the customer's points are displayed as a reward value.

Can I turn off notifications?+

Yes. Optional push notifications can be controlled through the app (where available) or your iPhone settings. Turning them off does not automatically delete messages already in your inbox.

How do I delete an account or membership?+

For customers, use the relevant business Profile screen to delete that membership or use My Cards to delete the full wallet. For owners, visit Business Settings to request business account deletion. If you cannot access the app, email us for assistance.

I changed phones. Can I restore my account?+

A customer account may be tied to an anonymous app installation. Account recovery is not guaranteed after changing devices or reinstalling the app unless a supported recovery method is available.

Is the service free?+

Yes. The customer and business loyalty features described in our current Terms are free to download and use.
